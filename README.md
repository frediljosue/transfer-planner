# Transfer Planner

**Open the app: https://frediljosue.github.io/transfer-planner/**

A free planner for college transfer applications. Keep your universities, scholarships, documents, essays, financial aid, tasks and deadlines in one place, with no account and no sign-up.

## What it does

- **Overview:** overall progress, counts by status, what needs attention in the next 7 days, and what is coming up.
- **Universities:** one card per school with status, deadline, portal link and a requirements checklist you can edit. Each card also holds that school's essays, recommendations, transcripts, SAT or ACT requirement, transfer credit notes, aid and fee details.
- **Scholarships:** amount, deadline, eligibility, GPA, residency, need, documents, essay and status, with search, filter and sort.
- **Documents:** one reusable checklist, with each document marked for the universities it is used for.
- **Essays:** every essay for every school and scholarship in one view, with prompt, a word or character limit, a live count, status and deadline.
- **Financial Aid:** FAFSA, CSS Profile and other aid items, plus a cost comparison that shows net cost per year.
- **Tasks and Deadlines:** filters for Today, This week, Upcoming and Completed. Overdue items turn red and items due within 7 days turn amber.
- **Calendar:** a month view of every deadline, with one click Add to Google Calendar links and a downloadable calendar file for your phone, Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.
- **My Profile:** your major, GPA, transfer goal and career goal.

## Your data stays on your device

Everything you type is saved in your own browser on your own device. There is no server and no account, and nobody else can see your entries.

- Clearing your browser data erases your planner.
- Your phone and your laptop each keep a separate copy.
- Go to **My Profile** and use **Export JSON** now and then. Use **Import JSON** to restore a backup or move to another device. **Export CSV** gives you a spreadsheet.

## What it does not do

- It does not submit applications or connect to the Common App or any school portal.
- It does not look up deadlines or requirements. The starting checklist items and document names are generic, so edit them for each school and confirm every date on the school's own website.

## Use it offline

The whole app is the single file `index.html`. Open it in a browser and it works without a server.

The page loads one font from Google Fonts. Delete that one `<link>` line in `index.html` if you want it to make no outside request. It still works in a system font.
